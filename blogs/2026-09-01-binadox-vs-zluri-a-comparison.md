Binadox and Zluri are platforms for managing SaaS spend, but they differ in scope and focus. Zluri’s scope includes access-request workflows, approvals, and just-in-time access, and it pulls subscription data directly from applications. Binadox combines SaaS spend management with cloud cost management for AWS, Azure, GCP, DigitalOcean, and Hetzner.
